SECTION 5: QUESTIONS CLAUDE CANNOT ANSWER WELL
================================================================================

AVOID THESE (Claude will struggle):

Too Personal:
✗ "Do you think this is publishable?" (subjective)
✗ "Is my project important?" (value judgment)
✗ "Am I a good writer?" (meta-question)

Too Vague:
✗ "Make my proposal better" (needs specifics)
✗ "Organize my work" (too broad)
✗ "What should my book be about?" (your decision)

Requiring Judgment Calls:
✗ "Which university press should I submit to?" (career decision)
✗ "Should I prioritize this chapter?" (priority judgment)
✗ "Is this argument academically sound?" (field-specific expertise)

BETTER VERSIONS:

Instead of: "Make my proposal better"
Ask:       "Improve the structure: does the introduction clearly 
            preview the main argument? Does the chapter outline 
            flow logically from introduction to conclusion?"

Instead of: "Organize my work"
Ask:       "Create a folder structure for:
            - Final proposal
            - Chapter drafts by completion status
            - Research papers by subject
            - Theoretical frameworks by application"

Instead of: "Is this good?"
Ask:       "Analyze this argument for logical coherence. 
            Does each claim follow from the evidence? 
            Are there logical gaps I should address?"
